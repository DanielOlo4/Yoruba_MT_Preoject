    ❓ How to Use

    1️⃣ Choose Direction

    Select whether to translate from English to Yorùbá or Yorùbá to English using the buttons at the top.

    2️⃣ Enter Text

    Type or paste your text in the input box. You can enter classroom greetings, instructions, academic terms, and more.

    3️⃣ Click Translate

    Press the Translate button or use Ctrl + Enter to translate.

    4️⃣ Review Results

    View the translation along with confidence scores, match types, and alternative translations.

    5️⃣ Quick Examples

    Click any example sentence below the translator to instantly translate it.

    6️⃣ Batch Mode

    Enter multiple sentences (one per line) in the batch section for bulk translation.

    This system covers education and classroom vocabulary including greetings, instructions, subjects, school environment, numbers, days, and academic discussions.
